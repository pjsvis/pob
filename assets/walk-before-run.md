## 1. Intention is Pre-Tensioning (In-Tension)

In a standard crane-and-pulley system, nothing happens until the motor turns and a cable pulls a lever. It is purely reactive.

But a biological organism is a closed-loop, pre-stressed tensegrity system. Look at the vectored diagram above: the compression struts (bones) are completely suspended within a continuous web of tension (fascia and muscle).

Before you even take a step, **intention is the signal the CNS sends to pre-stress specific vectors in that web.**

Intention is the act of dialing the tension up or down across the network so that the structural integrity remains perfectly stable during the execution of a movement.

## 3. The Running Trap: Why Velocity Demands Resolution

You cannot bypass the walking phase because velocity multiplies the structural stakes.

* **Walking** is a low-velocity, low-impact sandbox. If your vector mapping is slightly blurry, the system can handle the minor misalignment because the kinetic loads are low. The CNS can make real-time adjustments without blowing out a circuit.
* **Running** is an explosion of kinetic forces. Every foot strike sends a wave of energy through the structure that can equal multiple times your body weight.

If you attempt to run with a degraded, low-resolution walking map—where sensory-motor amnesia has blacked out sections of your hips or torso—those massive force vectors have nowhere to go. They can't be distributed smoothly through the continuous web because the wires are offline. The force bottlenecks at a single joint, the vector shears across a bone, and the CNS hits the emergency brakes. You lock up into an Orange or Red state, and the fitness industry sells you a knee brace or an orthotic to fix a software failure.

The takeaway for the reader is clear: **Rehabilitation and movement mastery require returning to the toddler's sandbox.**
