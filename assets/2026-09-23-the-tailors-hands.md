## The perception, as stated

> The whole fascial change thing happens over different time scales. Small scale stuff is just aches, which can be diminished by pandiculation. Larger scale changes feel like being fitted for a suit — you are in someone else's hands, and these hands make one do weird things. But the trust is there, because you chose to go to the tailor.

## What it is: the phenomenology of the slow layer

The two-timescale argument is third-person: two material systems, six orders of magnitude apart, session → matrix → signal → scaffold. This perception is its first-person correlate, and the mapping is exact:

| | Fast layer (matrix, days) | Slow layer (scaffold, months) |
|---|---|---|
| What it feels like | Aches — yours to probe, diminish, converge on | Being fitted — someone else's hands |
| Agency | You act | You are acted upon |
| Instrument | Pandiculation (the search) | Trust (the only interface) |
| Telemetry | Live — afferent read during actuation | None — remodeling is below the perceptual floor |

**The agency phenomenology tracks the control structure.** Fast-layer degrees of freedom have a conscious actuation path — you can contract, release, position, search. Slow-layer degrees of freedom have none: no actuation exists for "remodel my collagen." The hands in the perception are the mechanotransduction machinery — fibroblasts reading the matrix signal, sustained, rebuilding the scaffold — rendered in first person as an *external agent*, because that is precisely what a process without conscious actuation looks like from inside. The weird things the hands make one do (the unwinding configurations, the positions arrived at rather than chosen) are the slow network's tension redistribution dragging the body through postures consciousness never issued.

This is why the aches and the fitting feel categorically different, not just different in magnitude: **one is acting, the other is being altered.** The body schema is transparent in the first and semi-opaque in the second — subject in the first, subject-and-object in the second.

## Trust: earned on the fast loop, spent on the slow one

Why is the trust warranted? Not naively — structurally. At the slow timescale there is no telemetry: you cannot watch collagen being rebuilt, and no afferent reports it. Verification in-process is unavailable, so procedural trust is the *only* interface the architecture offers. But the trust has a provenance: **you chose the tailor on the fast loop's track record.** The aches diminished; the search converged; the benchmark sessions softened. Every piece of evidence that justifies trusting the slow process was collected at the fast timescale, where measurement was possible. Trust is earned on the loop where you can see, and spent on the loop where you cannot. That is what "you chose to go to the tailor" cashes out as: the practice adoption *was* the choosing, made on fast-loop evidence.

## The two levels, joined to the search

Yesterday's asset: pandiculation is search on a landscape (hysteretic, path-dependent, local probing). This perception supplies the other level: **the fitting is the landscape migrating.** The slow remodel reparametrizes the energy landscape that the fast search runs on. Two-level structure:

> fast search (pandiculation, aches, live telemetry) on a landscape slowly reshaped by slow remodeling (the fitting, no telemetry, trust interface)

The recursive shape is the book's whole argument in miniature: you cannot command the structure; you can only search within it today and choose the signals that reshape it for tomorrow's searching.

## The caution the metaphor needs (Hume's Razor, applied to one's own tailor)

The suit metaphor smuggles in one thing it must not: the tailor's infallibility. Large-scale fascial change is not always the good kind — adhesions are also large-scale fascial change; the "hands" can fit you for a worse suit. And weird sensations are ambiguous between remodeling-work and warning. The check is post-hoc, on the loop where telemetry exists: the floor benchmark sessions are the **fitting reviews**. Trust the process between reviews, but hold the review — if the benchmark hardens rather than softens over weeks while the "fitting" continues, the hands are not a tailor, and the trust is due for withdrawal. Trust with a stated withdrawal condition; otherwise it is not trust but abdication.
