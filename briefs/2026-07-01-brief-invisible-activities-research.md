# Brief: Research — Invisible Activities Explained Through Continuum Mechanics

**Date:** 2026-06-30
**Status:** Open
**Found during:** The walking-songs session, which led to the top-sight question "is the tensor/continuum exercise of use, and on which activities?" The floor-state piece (`content/floor-state.md`) is the template and the proof.

## Objective

Produce research priors and a narrativised bibliography for a set of culturally-independent, low-status human activities that yield to tensor-algebra / bounded-continuum-mechanics modelling — the "invisible activities detonated by the frame" through-line. Then determine placement: which activities belong in the book, which in standalone blog essays, and which to set down. The deliverable is the research base and the placement decision, not drafted chapters or essays.

## The through-line (and why the floor is the template)

The floor-state piece is the proof of concept. Lying on the floor — the most culturally trivial thing a body does — rendered as "a bounded continuum bio-tensegrity structure resting on a Euclidean half space in an orthogonal 1g vector field, internal strain E = 0, identity channels to the ground datum." Zero cultural sourcing, zero appropriation risk, and the frame makes a trivial activity non-obvious. Two properties compound:

1. **Culturally independent** — no sourcing gate, no yap entry. The frame speaks for itself. (The discipline condition.)
2. **The frame makes a low-status activity non-obvious** — the surprise *is* the value. (The sufficiency condition.)

The job is to find activities that pass both, and to be honest about which candidates fail one or both (and whether they survive as a different track — a blog essay, not a book chapter).

### Already in the book (subsumed — the receipts)

Walking/running, twisting, posture/idle, lifting (partially), repetitive strain, the one-inch punch / impact kinetic chain, the floor-state and upright-state. The frame works here; these prove it.

### Tier C — mechanism-real, cultural-sourcing is the weak link

Walking-songs (LRC + Poynting flux), marching cadence, drumming/dancing, work songs, pilgrimage. **Walking-songs stays as the filed assess-stage material** (`assets/walking-songs-poynting-findings.md`): the mechanism is sound, but it fails the floor test (needs a cultural frame; singing-while-walking is already "interesting"). It is a standalone-essay candidate, not a book-chapter candidate, and the cultural-sourcing gate stands. **Walking itself was a good stepping stone to get here — keep it, don't discard it.** The stepping-stone is part of the provenance, even though the through-line moved past it.

## What to research (the priors)

For each Tier A candidate, and for the Tier B candidates if placement is contested:

1. **The biomechanical prior.** Is the continuum model already established in the literature, or is it the book's synthesis? Which existing measurements (force plates, EMG, pressure mapping, motion capture, imaging) constrain the model? The floor-state piece draws on zero external priors because the model is definitional; load-carrying and massage will have real prior literature. Find it.
2. **The tensor frame's specific contribution.** What does the continuum model say that the existing sport-science / ergonomics / physical-therapy literature does not? This is the "is the exercise of use" test per candidate. If the continuum frame only re-labels what the field already says, the candidate is decorative — cut it or demote it to a blog illustration.
3. **The measurement boundary.** What is measured vs what is modelled. The book's epistemic status is "models, not measurements"; each candidate needs the same honesty. Where would the Poynting vector / stress field / prestress be *inferred* rather than *measured*? State it.
4. **The clinical / pathology extension.** The book's thesis is that pathology is stress-localization (non-zero divergence of stress, the cramp/clamp/guarding). Each candidate should connect to that thesis — load-carrying to overuse injury, massage to the release question, falling to injury-as-localization. The extension is what makes a chapter, not a vignette.

## Deliverables

1. **Research priors** — per candidate, the prior literature, the tensor frame's contribution, the measurement boundary, and the clinical extension. Held in a scratchpad or as an asset.
2. **A narrativised bibliography** — the sources, each entry explaining why it is cited and what it contributes (per the self-contained-essay playbook's bibliography style: Author (Year). *Title*. Publisher. Narrative explanation. No DOIs, no page numbers). The bibliography is the evidence the exercise is of use; it is also the gate against yap.
3. **A placement recommendation** — which candidates become book chapters, which become standalone blog essays, which are set down. The recommendation is *for review*, not final — we decide together (the curation loop's assess stage).

## Placement criteria (book vs blog vs set down)

- **Book chapter** if: culturally independent (floor test 1) AND the frame detonates a low-status activity (floor test 2) AND the clinical/pathology extension connects to the book's thesis AND the tensor frame's contribution is non-redundant with the existing literature. Tier A candidates are the likely book additions.
- **Standalone essay** if: mechanism-real but the cultural frame is load-bearing (fails floor test 1) OR the activity is already high-status and the piece's value is the synthesis rather than the detonation (fails floor test 2). Walking-songs is the filed instance. Tier B may land here.
- **Set down** if: the continuum frame only re-labels what the field already says (decorative), or the sourcing is intractable, or the book already covers it.

## Constraints

- **Investigate before touching the manuscript.** The perturbation process applies: research, assess, then decide placement. Do not draft a chapter or an essay until the research priors and the bibliography exist and the placement is agreed.
- **Cultural independence is the discipline condition.** Any candidate that requires a cultural sourcing pass to be interesting is a blog essay, not a book chapter — and even then, the gate from the walking-songs findings applies (every named tradition sourced or cut; mechanism universal, cultural reasons irreducible).
- **The Humean caveat applies.** Distinguish measured from modelled, and state the inference. Do not present a continuum model as an established measurement.
- **The top-sight is the point of the exercise.** The list and the placement recommendation are a top-sight into whether the tensor/continuum exercise is of use across activities, not just on the floor. If the research shows the frame is decorative on most candidates, that is a finding worth recording (and a reason to narrow the book's ambition), not a failure to paper over.
