The essay can carry that caveat without the book having to underwrite it. A chapter asserts; it cannot hedge its own evidence base without breaking its arc.

## Proposed Title

**The Unattended Protocol**

**Dek:** Lie on the floor for two hours and direct nothing. What survives of the explanations for what happens is a mechanism that turns on a six-orders-of-magnitude difference between the tissue a session can change and the tissue it cannot.

## Content Outline

1. **Hook** — the practice as it actually runs. Two hours on the floor, nothing consciously directed, a low wooden stool as the checkpoint. The reader's objection arrives immediately and is correct: nothing is being done. State it plainly, because the piece is an answer to it.

2. **What the practice does, stated precisely** — the floor as a large-area position datum with a weak pressure readout, not a calibration rig; there is no tension standard to calibrate against. The noise claim split: motor, attentional and reafference noise drop, while tissue stiffness *rises*, because resting muscle gets stiffer rather than softer (thixotropy, the short-range elastic component). That inversion forces the sequence — passive first, then active — and it is the first place the naive reading of the practice is wrong.

3. **The mechanism: two material systems on two clocks** — collagen half-life of order a century against hyaluronan's hours to days. The matrix is the tunable part; the scaffold is not. The matrix is also the signalling layer, which makes it the only handle on the scaffold: matrix state → cell signal → sustained response → scaffold. What "setting the configuration" means concretely — layer gliding, hydration, cross-link state. The prediction that follows: a change with a one-to-three-day decay, and therefore a practice rather than a repair.

4. **The sequence, and the three clocks** — ache reduction on the neural clock, range gain on the mechanical clock, the sounds on the interface clock. Why every reading of the practice collapses them into one causal chain, and why the sequence is a sequence and not a chain.

5. **The sounds, and the three explanations that did not survive** — cavitation (wrong sound class), stick-slip (superseded by the slip-pulse result), guard release (right intuition, wrong causal slot: a guard is a policy, and policies do not emit acoustic emissions). What survives: opening slip pulses at soft–rigid interfaces; the tonal/broadband diagnostic that distinguishes a confined pathway from a flat one; cascades as crackling noise; and the end of a cascade as a state marker — an *observable* termination criterion, which is the only kind of directive worth following.

6. **The nutrition and the duration, graded** — salt strongest, protein moderate, copper missing from the list, vitamin C plausible. The appetite science is weaker than the biochemistry. Open-ended duration is defensible for the same reason the protocol works: the sub-cortical loop is a functioning regulator.

7. **Resolution** — *follow the ache, record the story, let the stool be the measurement.* The practice is a practice, not a therapy. The piece ends on the three falsifiers it cannot run: the heavy-tail tally of the sounds, the surface-EMG test that separates an interface slip from a guard release, and the re-impose-guarding test that separates the two readings of the range gain.

## What this closes

Three open items in `briefs/2026-07-13-brief-the-floor-as-calibration-datum.md`:

- **Line 63, the active-vs-passive tension.** That brief left it as a question — does active efforting interfere with the passive idle-state redraw, or complement it? The thixotropy result answers it: resting muscle *increases* stiffness, so the sequence is forced. Passive first, because movement is what breaks the cross-bridges. The two are not alternatives; they are ordered.
- **Line 111, the consolidation question** — "does the calibrated state consolidate or fade — decides whether re-grounding on the datum is therapy or practice." The two-timescale argument answers it: practice. The fast layer decays in days; the scaffold does not move.
- **Line 110's prediction is inverted.** That brief proposed the passive-only test on the assumption that passive rest restores and active pandiculation adds. The thixotropy finding reverses the first half.

## Research needed

- [ ] **The heavy-tail tally.** Log the sounds by amplitude (barely audible / clear / loud) within and across sessions and look at the distribution shape. Only the shape separates avalanche dynamics from progressive lubrication; the decay alone does not discriminate.
- [ ] **The EMG test.** Surface EMG over the region during a cascade. An EMG drop coincident with the sound supports the guard-release reading; an unchanged trace supports the interface reading. The two predict oppositely, which is what makes it decisive.
- [ ] **The re-impose-guarding test.** After a new range appears, voluntarily co-contract the region. Range retreats → guard-limited. Range holds → interface-limited.
- [ ] **Verify Oster & Jaffe's frequency figure against the primary text.** The *Biophys J* full text was paywalled. The low-frequency character of muscle sound is well attested; the exact band was taken from secondary sources.
- [ ] **Verify the manipulation-literature findings against a primary source.** The "audible sound is not an indicator of success" conclusion was taken from a trade summary of Sillevis's work, not from the papers.
- [ ] **Check the adjacent asset cluster for overlap** — `assets/2026-08-21-floor-work-as-mechanical-reset.md`, `assets/maintenance-burden-equation.md`, `assets/sleep-tension-and-structural-lockup.md`, `assets/second-sleep-and-prestress-hypothesis.md`, `assets/the-geometry-of-rest.md`. Several of these may be one essay rather than five.

## Open decisions

1. **The rug.** `briefs/chapter-15-the-floor-datum.md:18` names the practice space "The Rug Sandbox." A rug is compliant *and* insulating — it smears the pressure distribution, which is the only readout of the internal tension field, and it retains the warmth the thixotropic argument wants to accumulate. It is optimised for the wrong half. Decide whether ch15's rug stays, becomes a mat, or becomes a bare floor with the rug named as the error.
2. **The essay's second half.** The adjudication of the sounds is the most honest part of the material and the least book-shaped. If the essay is cut for length, the adjudication is what to protect; the nutrition section is what to cut.
3. **The chapter.** Route A (ch15 section) or Route B (new chapter) — decided after the essay, with an ADR if Route B.
