# Penrose Diagrams

:::newthought
Penrose diagrams are conformal maps that capture the causal structure of spacetimes while compressing infinite distances. The impossible objects below are illustrations; the bio‑tensegrity diagrams are rendered by the hybrid ASCII→SVG pipeline (ADR‑002).
:::

## Penrose Triangle (illustration)

```text
   /\
  /  \
 /----\
 \    /
  \  /
   \/
   →
```

The arrow indicates the direction of the optical illusion; the shape is a classic "impossible" triangle. Rendered as a `<pre>` block — this is line art, not a tensor diagram.

## Penrose Staircase (illustration)

```text
   ┌─────┐
   │     │
   │  ──►│
   │     │
   └─────┘
```

The arrow inside the staircase illustrates the perpetual ascent illusion. Also a `<pre>` illustration.

# Bio‑Tensegrity

:::newthought
Bio‑tensegrity models cells and tissues as a network of tension‑bearing cables and compression‑bearing rods. The diagrams below are rendered by the hybrid ASCII→SVG pipeline: `o`/`□`/`△` are tensor nodes, `|`/`—` are legs, and `◇`/`Δ`/`⊚` are matrix stamps. The half‑space (`---`) is the Euclidean plane — the floor — with the 1‑g field pointing down into the material.
:::

## Simple Bio‑Tensegrity Unit

```
↓ ↓ ↓

---
```
