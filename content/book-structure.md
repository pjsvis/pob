# The Poverty of Bio-Mechanics

*Peter J Smith · 27 June 2026*

*A staved finger is not a broken finger — it is a lost one. It hurts because the nervous system has lost its reference, and steepling the hand gives it back. This book extends that principle to the entire human frame.*

---

## Structure

The book has four parts, 21 chapters, and one appendix. Each chapter follows the same arc: a familiar sensation the reader recognises from their own body, a mathematical justification grounded in continuum mechanics, and — where applicable — a hardware smoke test the reader can run on themselves.

The Popperian Falsification Protocols have been moved to **Appendix A**. They are for researchers who want to test the model under controlled conditions. The main text is for practitioners who want to understand why their body hurts and what to do about it.
