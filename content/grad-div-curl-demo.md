## 3. Grad / Div / Curl Narratives

```markdown
:::grad
The gradient of a scalar field $f$ points in the direction of greatest increase and has magnitude equal to the rate of increase.
:::
```

```markdown
:::div
The divergence of a vector field $\mathbf{F}$ measures the net flux density out of an infinitesimal volume.
:::
```

```markdown
:::curl
The curl of a vector field $\mathbf{F}$ describes the local rotation, with direction given by the right‑hand rule.
:::
```
