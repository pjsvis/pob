# Part IV: The Detective Case Files

The last four chapters are case files. Each one takes a common injury — a broken collarbone, a sprained ankle, carpal tunnel syndrome, plantar fasciitis — and re-frames it as a deliberate engineering decision by the body's protective system, not a failure.

The clavicular fuse: the collarbone breaks so the cervical spine doesn't. The lateral ankle fuse: the ankle ligaments tear so the knee hinge doesn't. The carpal tunnel vice: the wrist burns because the elbow lost its reference. The plantar shard: the heel hurts in the morning because a locked lower back is pulling the line from the other end.

These are detective stories. The injury is the crime scene. The pain is the witness. The culprit is always upstream — a constraint elsewhere in the chain that forced the local structure to take the hit. The tensor algebra is the forensic toolkit that traces the load path back to its source.

Read these in any order. Each is self-contained. But each assumes the vocabulary of Parts I and III.
