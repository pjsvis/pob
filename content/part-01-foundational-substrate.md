# Part I: The Foundational Substrate

The first six chapters establish the ground — literally. The floor is the zero-load reference state, the datum against which everything else is measured. From that datum, the torsion engine of the spine, the crystalline grid of impact dynamics, the autonomous governor of the sub-cortical nervous system, the idle state that maps the frontier of safe movement, and the re-ingested walk that carries the floor's calibration into locomotion.

These chapters are the substrate because every subsequent chapter assumes them. The geometric hubs of Part II sit on top of this foundation. The runtime security layer of Part III governs it. The detective case files of Part IV diagnose what happens when it fails.

Read these chapters in order. They build a vocabulary — strain tensors, holonomic constraints, the Lie derivative, the Green/Yellow/Orange/Red taxonomy — that the rest of the book uses without re-explanation.
