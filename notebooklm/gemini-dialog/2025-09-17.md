### Common Uses
DisplayPort cables are primarily used to transmit digital video and audio from a source, like a computer or graphics card, to a display, such as a monitor. They are especially popular in PC gaming for their ability to support high resolutions and high refresh rates. 🖥️


It's often confused with an HDMI connector, but HDMI has a more symmetrical, trapezoidal shape with two beveled corners.
