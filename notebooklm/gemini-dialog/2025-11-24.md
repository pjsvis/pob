The problem, in the UK, is humidity. Get yourself a dehumidifier, lower humidity, and you'll notice a significant difference in heating bills and your comfort. This will be much better than just adding kitchen foil or fans (I think it's a gimmick). Lower humidity below from above high 65-70% (anything above 70% is mould & condensation friendly environment) to what is considered comfort zone of 40-50% (whatever level in this range you like). UK buildings are moisture traps, controlling humidity really affects how long you need to run your heating system to heat your house to the same temperature. It reduces problems with condensation, improves comfort etc, it definitely offsets in the long run extra energy needed to run the dehumidifier. I've tested this over the past years and my heating bills are quite low, I use much less gas for heating than in the past.
